    Behaviour

    Writing queries

    Any Gmail search works. Some useful operators:

    from:
    sender address or name
    to: / cc:
    recipients
    label:
    a label, e.g. label:clients
    is:unread
    also is:starred, is:important
    category:
    primary, promotions, updates…
    newer_than:
    7d, 2m, 1y
    has:attachment
    also filename:pdf
    OR - ( )
    combine, exclude, group

    Add in:inbox if you only want mail that is still in your inbox.

    Your tabs sync through your Chrome profile. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
